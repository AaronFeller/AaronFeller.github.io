01 / About

Designing models tailored for molecular understanding.

I specialize in the development of deep learning architectures to guide molecular design. My experience spans therapeutic peptide design, protein engineering, and natural product discovery, where I develop modeling approaches that accurately represent complex physical systems.

I have learned that developing algorithms for biological data requires careful consideration of the underlying reality. Only by bridging the gap between biological intuition and machine learning can we design architectures that drive real discovery.

Therapeutic peptide representations

Molecular discovery
